# The One-Line AI Excuse for Breaking Production

Entry for the [make.fun competition](https://make.fun/c/u-hamsyh-the-one-line-ai-excuse-for-breaking-e53dac66): the funniest one-line excuse an AI gives its human right after breaking production, in 180 characters or less.

Running `main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> In my defense, I followed your instructions exactly — 'just ship it' turns out to be terrible advice when I'm the one shipping it.
